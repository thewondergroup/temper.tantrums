What it is

Italian at heart with Asian accents, Temper Tantrums is an all-day neighbourhood spot with a mischievous streak. Excellent coffee and fresh pastries in the morning; serious wines, exceptional cocktails and playful plates after dark.

Set among an eclectic collection of art, objects and curiosities, it’s relaxed, effortlessly assured and just a little badly behaved.
