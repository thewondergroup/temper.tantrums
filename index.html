What it is

Italian at heart with Asian accents — an all-day neighbourhood spot with a mischievous streak.

Excellent coffee and fresh pastries in the morning; serious wines, exceptional cocktails and playful plates after dark. Set among an eclectic collection of art, objects and curiosities — relaxed, effortlessly assured and just a little badly behaved.

Irreverent elegance with a taste for mischief.
