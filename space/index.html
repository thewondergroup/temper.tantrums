The space

Collected, not decorated

An eclectic collection of art, objects and curiosities — relaxed, effortlessly assured and just a little badly behaved.

Look up

A ceiling of rock & roll

Chandeliers hang beneath a ceiling of framed faces — the loudest guests in the room, permanently installed. Below them, collected mirrors, old clocks and deep red leather.

At the bar

Serious bottles, soft lamplight

Wine racked to the ceiling, glasses overhead, stools pulled up to sage-green panelling. Espresso in the morning; by evening the same seat works just as hard for a negroni.

Café by day. Wine & cocktails by night.

Settle in

Built for staying

Leather sofas, odd lamps, herringbone floors and tables that have clearly lived a little. Come for a coffee, stay until the lights go down.

Like the room? Take it over.
